PRIVACY POLICY

Effective October 28, 2026

Who runs this site

The Visionary Project (www.thevisionaryproject.com) is the personal professional portfolio of an industrial engineer, enterprise sales professional, and technology builder, operated under AppsArchitects Holdings. Questions: hello@appsarchitects.com.

Analytics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the portfolio is used. It does not use cookies. It records:

  • which pages and sections are viewed (for example, which tunnel was entered);
  • the referring website, approximate country, browser, operating system, and device type;
  • anonymous interactions such as opening the contact panel or following a project link.

Visits are grouped using a hash that is discarded after 24 hours and is not stored as an identifier. The data cannot be used to identify you, and it is not used to track you across other websites.

What is not collected

  • No contact forms, accounts, or database. Nothing you type is stored by this site.
  • No advertising pixels, session recording, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking.
  • No attempt is made to identify individual visitors.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes technical request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Contact options & links

  • The email options open your own email app with a pre-filled subject. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.
  • "Copy email address" places the address on your clipboard on your device only.
  • LinkedIn and the project websites are third-party sites with their own privacy policies.

Your choices

Content blockers that block analytics scripts will prevent your visit from being counted; the site works the same either way. To ask about this policy, email hello@appsarchitects.com.
